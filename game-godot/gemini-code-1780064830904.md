# Patch Notes & Implementation Guide: Grabbable Cube Mechanics

This document outlines the strict fixes required for the grabbable cube interactions, including portal teleportation, collision integrity, interaction areas, and holding offsets. 

---

## 1. Portal Teleportation (While Holding)
**Issue:** The cube does not enter or teleport through portals while actively held by the player.
**Root Cause:** When an object is grabbed, it is often parented to the player or has its physics disabled (`isKinematic = true`), which prevents it from triggering portal colliders independently.
**Fix Implementation:**
* **Decouple on Teleport:** When the cube's collider intersects with the Portal Trigger, the script must check if the object is currently held.
* **Logic Flow:**
    1. `OnTriggerEnter(Portal)` detects the cube.
    2. Check `Cube.IsHeld`. If true, temporarily break the joint or unparent the cube.
    3. Calculate the rotational and positional offset relative to the exit portal.
    4. Teleport the cube.
    5. **Crucial Step:** Automatically re-establish the grab connection (snap back to hand/joint) at the destination so the player doesn't drop it, OR force the player to drop it upon entering the portal, depending on desired game design.

## 2. Strict Anti-Clipping (World Collision)
**Issue:** The held cube clips through walls and world geometry.
**Root Cause:** Updating the cube's position by modifying its `Transform.position` or directly parenting it to the camera/hand forces it through geometry, overriding the physics engine.
**Fix Implementation:**
* **Physics-Based Grabbing:** Never use `Transform.SetParent` for held items that need collision. 
* **Joint Connection:** Use a physics joint (e.g., `FixedJoint`, `SpringJoint`, or `ConfigurableJoint`) to attach the cube to the player's hold point.
* **Rigidbody Settings:** * Set the cube's Rigidbody Collision Detection to **Continuous Dynamic** to prevent it from phasing through thin walls at high speeds.
    * Ensure the holding script uses `Rigidbody.MovePosition()` and `Rigidbody.MoveRotation()` rather than `Transform` manipulation if not using joints.

## 3. Interaction Area Fixes ("E" Press & Scaling)
**Issue:** Area of interaction works initially when the cube is large, but fails after the second "E" press.
**Root Cause:** Scaling the object might be scaling the trigger colliders disproportionately, or the interaction state machine is not resetting correctly upon releasing the cube.
**Fix Implementation:**
* **State Reset:** Ensure that when "E" is pressed the second time (to drop/release), the boolean `isHeld` is set to `false` and the interaction Raycast/OverlapBox is re-enabled.
* **Scale Independent Triggers:** If the cube changes size, ensure the Interaction Trigger (a separate, invisible collider dedicated solely to detecting the player's "E" press) is a child object with an absolute scale, preventing it from shrinking or breaking when the parent cube scales.
* **Layer Mask Check:** Ensure dropping the cube doesn't accidentally move it to an "Ignore Raycast" layer that isn't reverted.

## 4. Hand Interaction Hitbox Fix
**Issue:** The area of interaction is not detecting the player's physical/VR hands.
**Root Cause:** The collision matrix is ignoring the "Hand" layer, or the hands lack the proper trigger colliders/rigidbodies to register an overlap with the cube's interaction zone.
**Fix Implementation:**
* **Collider Setup:** Attach a `SphereCollider` (set to `IsTrigger`) and a `Rigidbody` (set to `IsKinematic`) to the physical hand objects.
* **Physics Matrix:** Verify in your project settings that the **Hand** layer and the **Interactable** layer are set to collide/interact.
* **Script Logic:** Use `OnTriggerEnter` or an `Physics.OverlapSphere` cast originating from the hand's transform rather than the center of the camera.

## 5. Player Offset & Button Interaction
**Issue:** The cube must be held *below* the player but still interact with physical buttons and the world.
**Root Cause:** Standard grab scripts lock the object to the center of the screen/camera, lifting it too high. Disabling physics to hold it prevents button presses.
**Fix Implementation:**
* **Hold Anchor Setup:** Create an empty GameObject attached to the player rig, positioned lower down (e.g., at waist or knee height). Set *this* specific transform as the target for the cube to follow when grabbed.
* **Active Colliders:** Keep the cube's primary `BoxCollider` active while held. 
* **World Interaction:** Ensure the buttons in the world are triggered by standard physics collisions (`OnCollisionEnter` or `OnTriggerEnter`). Since the cube is being dragged by a physics joint or `MovePosition` (as fixed in Step 2), its collider will sweep through space and successfully press buttons on the floor or walls while dangling below the player.
